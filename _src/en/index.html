What is a living lab?

A real-world space, not just a lab experiment

A living lab is a place where residents, researchers and institutions jointly design, test and improve practices such as urban growing, composting and reuse of organic waste, or short supply chains.

Data collected in each lab feeds into a shared knowledge hub, so that solutions that work can be picked up by other cities too.

How the project works
Bucharest Living Lab

Farms around the capital, made more circular and inclusive

In Bucharest, the living lab is not a garden in the city centre but a network of peri-urban farms in the Bucharest-Ilfov area. Together with the farmers, the University of Bucharest team works to make the farms more circular, more efficient and more open to urban markets and to vulnerable people.
