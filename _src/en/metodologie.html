Deliverable D3.1

Data collection protocol

The protocol sets which data are collected from the living labs, in what format, how often and how they are transmitted. All partners collect the same data so that results can be compared across cities and transferred to other EU cities.

ElementWhat it sets
Type of dataWhat is collected: socio-economic, bio-physical, agro-ecological, climate, political and institutional data.
FormatHow the data are recorded, in a form common to all labs.
FrequencyHow often each type of data is collected.
TransmissionHow data reach the central Knowledge Hub and the dashboard.

Deliverable D3.4 · Activity 3.4

Site selection tool

The tool assesses how suitable cadastral units and informal plots are for (peri)urban agriculture. It is based on a qualitative response model that predicts expert assessments from georeferenced biophysical and socio-economic characteristics.

TypeCriterionSource on this site
BiophysicalSoilTo be completed
BiophysicalLand useAgriculture tab (land fund)
BiophysicalWater availabilityClimate tab (precipitation)
Socio-economicVicinity to marketsTo be completed
Socio-economicWomen’s safetyExpert assessment
Socio-economicAccessibility for people with disabilitiesExpert assessment
How it works

From expert assessment to the suitability map

  1. 1. Describing sites

    Cadastral units and informal plots, with georeferenced characteristics.

  2. 2. Expert assessment

    Experts assess the suitability of a sample of sites.

  3. 3. The model

    The model predicts expert assessments from site characteristics.

  4. 4. Baseline map

    Scaled to all sites, it produces a suitability baseline map.

  5. 5. Interventions

    Shows how suitability changes after interventions: soil, water, safe entrances.

The tool was developed and tested in an earlier project on allotment gardening in Benin and is adapted to the FEED4FOOD target groups. The deliverable is an Excel tool tailored to each city.

Deliverable D3.3 · Activity 3.3

Environmental footprint and life cycle analysis

The environmental impact indicators of the dashboard come from a life cycle analysis (LCA) that follows all material flows, from inputs to final outputs.

Energy and waste

Energy use, generation and re-use of organic waste.

Growing and processing

Chemicals used in plant growing, processing and cleaning of facilities.

Storage and transport

Carbon emissions of storing and transporting vegetables and fruit.

Activity 3.3.1

Product footprint: water and carbon

For transport, the emission is estimated as mass × distance × emission factor. The footprint calculator is in preparation and will use published emission factors, with sources and limits shown.
