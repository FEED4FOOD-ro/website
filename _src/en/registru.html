Air quality

PM10, PM2.5, NO₂ and O₃ concentrations at Bucharest stations, 2013–2025: verified data reported by Romania to the European Environment Agency.

Values by period

Verified (E1a) data reported by Romania (ANPM / RNMCA) to the EEA, Bucharest stations B1–B6 and B9: urban background (B1, B9), industrial (B2, B4, B5), traffic (B3, B6). Monthly means are computed from daily values (or hourly values with at least 18 valid hours per day); a month is left empty if fewer than 75% of its days are valid, and the yearly mean needs at least 9 valid months. Not every station measures every pollutant, and B9 has data from 2022. The red line shows the annual limit value in Directive 2008/50/EC (PM10 and NO₂: 40 µg/m³, PM2.5: 25 µg/m³).

Source: EEA Air Quality Download Service.

Crop water footprint

Calculate the water footprint of a crop per kilogram of product, in the same style as a carbon footprint: green water (rain), blue water (irrigation) and grey water (the water needed to dilute nitrogen lost to the environment). Choose the country, the crop and the planting date; the default yield is the INS average for Bucharest–Ilfov.

Total footprint–litres of water per kilogram
Green water–rain used by the plant
Blue water–irrigation needed
Grey water–diluting lost nitrogen

How it is calculated

  • Crop water need is calculated day by day as ETc = Kc × ET0, using the crop coefficients Kc and stage lengths of FAO-56 (Allen et al., 1998, Tables 11 and 12).
  • Green water is the smaller of ETc and effective rain; blue water is the difference up to ETc (irrigated crop only); grey water is α × N / (cmax − cnat), with α = 10% of nitrogen lost, cmax = 10 mg N/L and cnat = 0.
  • The footprint (litres per kg) is the water used per hectare (m³/ha) divided by the yield (t/ha), following the Water Footprint Network manual (Hoekstra et al., 2011).

Assumptions and limits

  • ET0 and precipitation are 1991–2020 monthly averages from weather reanalyses (Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0), not measurements, computed for one or two points in each country (see under the Country field), so they do not represent the whole country; effective rain is computed monthly with the USDA SCS method with no soil water store, so for a rain-fed crop the result is a lower bound.
  • FAO-56 coefficients and stage lengths are typical values, not adapted locally; where FAO gives a range for the final Kc, its midpoint is used.
  • The default yield is the 2021–2025 INS TEMPO average (AGR110A) for the Bucharest–Ilfov Region, irrigated and rain-fed together. For Cyprus, Greece and Poland, enter the local yield.
  • Default nitrogen rates are illustrative: replace them with the actual rate.
  • It does not include water used in processing, transport, packaging or greenhouses, and results are indicative, not an audit.

Sources

  • Hoekstra, A.Y., Chapagain, A.K., Aldaya, M.M., Mekonnen, M.M. (2011). The Water Footprint Assessment Manual: Setting the Global Standard. Earthscan, London. waterfootprint.org
