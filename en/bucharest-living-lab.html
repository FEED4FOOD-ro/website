Bucharest Living Lab

Farms around Bucharest becoming more circular, efficient and inclusive

Unlike the labs in Strovolos and Drama, the Bucharest lab is not a garden in the middle of the city but a network of peri-urban farms in the Bucharest-Ilfov area.

Why farms around the city?

Bucharest-Ilfov is one of Romania's most dynamic regions, and the city is quickly spreading over the surrounding farmland. Our research shows how strongly urban expansion is changing land use around the capital, and how climate change is increasing crop water requirements in the north of the region.

Farms near the city are the most accessible source of fresh vegetables for Bucharest residents, but they need support to stay viable and sustainable.

The University of Bucharest team works with farmers to change the way the farms are organised and to bring fresh local food closer to urban consumers.

What we aim for

  • More circular farms: composting and waste reuse within the lab.
  • More efficient farms: farmers sharing good practices.
  • More inclusive farms: better access to urban markets and bringing vulnerable people into the workforce.
  • A shared action plan for expanding agricultural areas, drawn up with the farmers, so that fresh and processed produce reaches urban consumers.
How it works

Three years, three stages

  1. 2024

    Preparation

    We assessed community needs and vegetable production in Bucharest-Ilfov, reviewed the literature and studied other living labs in Romania (West University of Timișoara, Alexandru Ioan Cuza University of Iași, Politehnica University of Bucharest).

  2. 2025

    The lab in operation

    Preparation and operating guides, the business model and the lab handbook. We learned from the urban micro-farms of Nicosia, Cyprus.

  3. 2026

    Learning hub

    We collect data from the lab, build the dashboard, analyse product footprints and prepare training materials.

Part of a European network

Four living labs, one knowledge hub

Data from all the labs flows into a shared knowledge hub coordinated by Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. A dashboard shows how each lab is doing, so that what works in one city can be taken up by others.

RO

Bucharest, Romania

Peri-urban farms in the Bucharest-Ilfov area. Funded by UEFISCDI, coordinated by the University of Bucharest.

CY

Strovolos, Cyprus

A lab in Acropolis Park, in a semi-arid urban setting: a greenhouse, raised beds, a shade-net house and a meeting area. Organic and regenerative agriculture.

GR

Drama, Greece

Urban gardens and composting, focused on women, young people who have left school, and migrants. Coordinated by Democritus University of Thrace and GERi Lab, with support from the Ladies Union of Drama.

PL

Gdańsk, Poland

The fourth living lab in the network, focused on local food production.
