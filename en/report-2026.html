2026 scientific report

Contract no. 50/2024, stage no. 3/2026. Scientific description of the stage results and the degree to which objectives were met.

English translation. The official report is in Romanian.

The project Vulnerable communities fostering innovation and governance of sustainable food systems in European cities (FEED4FOOD), run by the University of Bucharest, has a 3-year implementation period. The third stage (2026) is called Learning Hub. Coordinator: University of Bucharest.

1. Website with the dashboard

The dashboard is published on this website, on the Dashboard page. It brings together:

  • the registry of INS TEMPO agricultural indicators for Bucharest–Ilfov, with charts and values by year;
  • climate series from the ANM stations in Bucharest and Ilfov (temperature, precipitation, wind, pressure), monthly, yearly and 1991–2020 normals;
  • air quality series (PM10, PM2.5, NO₂, O₃) at the Bucharest stations, 2013–2025, plus a catalogue of open sources;
  • Excel export for every table and chart.

2. Data collection protocol

Deliverable D3.1 sets out what data is collected from the living labs, in what format, how often and how it is transmitted, so that results can be compared across cities. Details are on the Methodology page.

3. Selection tool

Deliverable D3.4 assesses how suitable cadastral units and informal plots are for (peri-)urban agriculture, using a model that predicts expert ratings from georeferenced biophysical and socio-economic characteristics. It is described on the Methodology page.

4. Methodological report on the instruments

The report brings together the method, formulas, data sources and limits of each instrument: context, protocol, indicators and dashboard, life cycle assessment and footprint, site selection, sources and licences. Its structure is presented on the Methodology page.

5. Training and documentation materials

The project's training and documentation materials are grouped under “Guides and resources” on the Results page. Training materials are being prepared for using the site selection tool.

6. Final report

The stage's final report summarises the results above. This page is its public version and is completed as results are finalised.
