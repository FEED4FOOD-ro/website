The FEED4FOOD consortium

Coordinator The Netherlands

Amsterdam Centre for World Food Studies (ACWFS), Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam

An interdisciplinary institute that carries out research together with societal stakeholders and end users to improve food and nutrition security. It looks for policies and strategies that are economically, socially and ecologically sustainable, starting from the view that food security depends on economic, agro-ecological, technological, health and cultural factors. ACWFS coordinates the project and leads the central knowledge and learning hub.

  • Dr Lia van Wesenbeeck, project coordinator, associate professor of development economics, director of ACWFS
  • Prof. Dr Patricia Lago, principal investigator, leads the Software and Sustainability (S2) group, Department of Computer Science
  • Dr Ben Sonneveld, principal investigator, associate professor at the Athena Institute, deputy director of ACWFS
  • Klervie Toczé, postdoctoral researcher
Romania

University of Bucharest

With a history of more than 150 years, the University of Bucharest fosters a dynamic and inclusive academic environment, focused on student-centred learning supported by creativity, innovation and pragmatism. It coordinates the Bucharest Living Lab.

Greece

Democritus University of Thrace (DUTh) and GERi Lab

Founded in 1973, it is the only university in the Thrace region, with campuses in Komotini, Xanthi, Alexandroupoli and Orestiada, more than 29,000 students and over 700 academic staff.

The Geomorphology, Edaphology and Riparian Areas Lab (GERi Lab), part of the Department of Forestry and Natural Environment Sciences, implements nature-based solutions for the sustainable management of natural resources, contributing to the European Green Deal and the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

  • Assoc. Prof. George Zaimes, project manager, director of GERi Lab and deputy director of the UNESCO Chair Con-E-Ect
Greece

Ladies Union of Drama

An organisation providing social and humanitarian activities in the community, with a special focus on vulnerable groups. Its mission is to help resolve humanitarian and social crises.

  • Aliki Tsiamoura, project manager, president of the Ladies Union of Drama
  • Maria Papadopoulou, administrative support
Cyprus

KES Research Centre (KESRC)

An independent, non-profit research organisation based in Nicosia, focused on environmental and business sustainability.

  • Dr Dimitrios Sarris, project manager, research director, plant biologist and ecologist with more than 20 years of research experience
Cyprus

VL Sustainability Metrics Ltd (SuMe)

A start-up founded in 2022, providing consulting and training in environmental sustainability. It specialises in calculating, reporting and reducing the environmental footprint of products, companies, organisations and events.

  • Dr Vassilis Litskas, project leader
Cyprus

Strovolos Municipality

Established in 1986, it is the second-largest municipality in Cyprus, known for its commitment to sustainability. It hosts the Strovolos Living Lab.

  • Eleni Andreou, environmental officer, agronomist
  • Maria Tsangari, municipal officer for European programmes
