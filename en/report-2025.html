Results · Stage 2

2025 scientific report

Contract no. 50/2024, stage no. 2/2025. Scientific description of the stage results and the degree to which objectives were met.

English translation. The official report is in Romanian.

The project Vulnerable communities fostering innovation and governance of sustainable food systems in European cities (FEED4FOOD), run by the University of Bucharest, has a 3-year implementation period. The activities planned for stage two (2025) are:

Activities
  1. Living lab operating guide (preparation guide, operating guide)
  2. Participation in two conferences
  3. Publication of two articles
  4. Living lab business model
  5. Handbook for a successful living lab

1. Living lab operating guide

Activity planned for the 2025 stage: the living lab preparation guide and operating guide.

2. Conference participation

Workshop “Innovation and Governance in Food Systems”, 21–23 March 2025, Bucharest

The annual meeting of the FEED4FOOD partners, hosted by the University of Bucharest.

FEED4FOOD at EPAM 2025, Shanghai

Dr George Zaimes of Democritus University of Thrace (DUTh), Greece, presented FEED4FOOD at the 15th International Conference on Environmental and Public Health Issues in Asian Mega-cities (EPAM 2025) in Shanghai, China.

His presentation, “Food Sustainability & Climate Resilience through Urban Gardens”, highlighted how FEED4FOOD promotes inclusive, sustainable and community-oriented urban food systems, empowering vulnerable groups and improving climate resilience in European cities. At the event, Dr Zaimes also distributed the FEED4FOOD brochure.

4. Living lab business model

To develop business models specific to living labs, we travelled to our partners in Cyprus. We visited two urban micro-farms in Nicosia, where fresh-water scarcity is a major challenge and efficient management of this essential resource remains a key issue for urban agriculture in the region.

Many thanks to our hosts, Vassilis Litskas, Special Scientist at Cyprus University of Technology – Department of Agricultural Sciences, Biotechnology and Food Science, and Dimitrios Sarris, Research Director at KES Research Centre, for their hospitality and a very valuable exchange of knowledge!
