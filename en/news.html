What's happening at FEED4FOOD

Events, study visits, conferences and new publications.

13–14 November 2026 · Bucharest · Conference

Two FEED4FOOD papers at GEOPerspectives 2026

The team presented two papers at the 2nd International Conference “Geographical Perspectives on Global Changes” (GEOPerspectives), Bucharest.

“Employment Dynamics in the Agricultural Economy: Evidence from the Bucharest-Ilfov Development Region”
Daniel Constantin Diaconu, Andrei Rafael Gruia, Andreea Karina Gruia, Alexandra Grecu, Cristian Constantin Drăghici, Daniel Peptenatu.

“Urban–Rural Territorial Change and the Pressure of Accelerated Urbanization on Agricultural Land in the Bucharest-Ilfov Region”
Andrei-Rafael Gruia, Daniel Peptenatu.

1–3 October 2026 · Cluj-Napoca, Romania · Conference

Two FEED4FOOD papers at TICPA 2026

The team presented two papers at the 6th Transylvanian International Conference in Public Administration (TICPA 2026), held at Babeș-Bolyai University (FSPAC), Cluj-Napoca, on the theme “Public Administration and the Future of Democracy: Institutions, Innovation, and Public Value”.

“Employment Dynamics in the Agricultural Economy: Evidence from the Bucharest-Ilfov Development Region”

Panel: Public economics and finance, 2 October 2026, 14:00–16:00.

“The Cost of Growth: Quantifying the Pressure of Accelerated Urbanization on Agricultural Land”
Andrei Rafael Gruia, Daniel Constantin Diaconu, Andreea Karina Gruia, Daniel Peptenatu (University of Bucharest).
Panel: PA Topics (4), 2 October 2026, 14:00–16:00.

10 June 2026 · Online

FEED4FOOD at the DUT session “From Project to Policy”

Dr George Zaimes (DUTh) spoke in the breakout session “Circular Urban Economies” of the Driving Urban Transitions (DUT) session From Project to Policy: On Urban Food Systems, Circularity, and Nature-based Solutions, 10 June 2026, 14:00.

11–12 June 2026 · Online

FEED4FOOD webinar and the NEB-FOODCITYBOOST satellite event

Webinar “Living Labs, Nature-Based Solutions and Community Action” — 12 June 2026, 10:00–13:00 CET, online. Experts, practitioners and communities from across Europe discussed urban agriculture, water harvesting, biodiversity-friendly food production, community participation and the role of living labs in urban resilience.

NEB-FOODCITYBOOST satellite event (New European Bauhaus) — 11 June 2026, 9:00 Brussels time, online. Project coordinator Lia van Wesenbeeck gave the guest presentation “Hand in hand for inclusive food security: Feed4Food’s Living Labs”, on how the living labs support inclusive, resilient, community-driven urban food systems.

11 May 2026 · Drama, Greece

Hands-on learning for sustainable urban gardening in Drama

GERi Lab organised an interactive FEED4FOOD workshop in the urban gardens of Drama for young participants. They explored soil texture, structure, colour, horizons and pH through simple field methods, building environmental awareness and practical gardening skills.

2026 · Strovolos and Nicosia, Cyprus

Living labs and schools growing communities together

Prof. Stalo Antoniou, a teacher at the Apostolos Varnavas Primary School in Nicosia, visits the FEED4FOOD living lab every two weeks with a group of 15 students aged 8–9. FEED4FOOD interviewed her about the experience.

October 2025 · Uganda project workshop

Bridging projects: NbS-4-Climate and FEED4FOOD

Dr George Zaimes also introduced FEED4FOOD at the NbS-4-Climate Teaching Tuning Workshop, a new Erasmus+ project promoting nature-based solutions in Uganda. Both initiatives share a commitment to sustainability, community engagement and climate resilience.

30 September – 1 October 2025 · Milan

FEED4FOOD at the Driving Urban Transitions Projects Event

Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (project leader Prof. Lia van Wesenbeeck) and AREA (Viviana Capurso) represented FEED4FOOD at the DUT Projects Event in Milan. The project poster drew great interest for its living lab model in Strovolos, Drama and Bucharest, which connects local authorities, SMEs and universities to co-create urban food solutions.

24 November 2025 · Publication

An article on the FEED4FOOD living labs in Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems

Consortium partners have published “Hand in hand for urban food security: Feed4Food's living labs and knowledge hubs in European cities”, presenting the project's living labs and knowledge hubs in European cities.

Authors: Vassilis D. Litskas (SuMe, Cyprus), Valasia Iakovoglou, Viviana Capourso, Daniel Constantin Diaconu (University of Bucharest) and Lia van Wesenbeeck (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam). Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems, vol. 9, 2025, article 1707753 (open access).

2026 · Publication

New article in Water

Daniel Constantin Diaconu and Dragoș Cristian Diaconu have published “Catchment Scale and Spatial Synchrony of Hydrological Drought in Serbia, 1991–2024: Evidence from Rescued Monthly Discharge Records” in Water 2026, vol. 18, no. 22, 2861 (MDPI, open access).

2025 · Nicosia, Cyprus

Study visit to the urban micro-farms of Nicosia

To develop the business model for the living labs, we visited our partners in Cyprus. We saw two urban micro-farms in Nicosia, where fresh-water scarcity is a major challenge and efficient management of this resource remains essential for urban farming in the region.

Many thanks to our hosts, Vassilis Litskas (Cyprus University of Technology, Department of Agricultural Sciences, Biotechnology and Food Science) and Dimitrios Sarris (Research Director, KES Research Centre), for their hospitality and for a very valuable exchange of knowledge!

2025 · Shanghai, China

FEED4FOOD at the EPAM 2025 conference

Dr George Zaimes of Democritus University of Thrace (DUTh), Greece, presented FEED4FOOD at the 15th International Conference on Environmental and Public Health Issues in Asian Mega-cities (EPAM 2025) in Shanghai.

His talk, “Food Sustainability & Climate Resilience through Urban Gardens”, showed how FEED4FOOD promotes inclusive, sustainable and community-oriented urban food systems that support vulnerable groups and strengthen the climate resilience of European cities. Dr Zaimes also handed out the project brochure.

21–23 March 2025 · Bucharest

Workshop “Innovation and Governance in Food Systems”

The University of Bucharest hosted the annual meeting of the FEED4FOOD partners. Over three days, researchers and partner representatives from all project countries discussed innovation and governance in urban food systems, and participants received certificates of attendance.

Facilitator: Assoc. Prof. Daniel Constantin Diaconu. Scientific committee: Daniel Peptenatu, Lia van Wesenbeeck, Magdalena Platis, Adrian Tișcovschi, Cristian Constantin Drăghici, Petre Brețcan.
