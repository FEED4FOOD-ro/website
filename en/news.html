24 November 2025 · Publication

An article on the FEED4FOOD living labs in Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems

Consortium partners have published “Hand in hand for urban food security: Feed4Food's living labs and knowledge hubs in European cities”, presenting the project's living labs and knowledge hubs in European cities.

Authors: Vassilis D. Litskas (SuMe, Cyprus), Valasia Iakovoglou, Viviana Capourso, Daniel Constantin Diaconu (University of Bucharest) and Lia van Wesenbeeck (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam). Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems, vol. 9, 2025, article 1707753 (open access).

2026 · Publication

New article in Water

Daniel Constantin Diaconu and Dragoș Cristian Diaconu have published “Catchment Scale and Spatial Synchrony of Hydrological Drought in Serbia, 1991–2024: Evidence from Rescued Monthly Discharge Records” in Water 2026, vol. 18, no. 22, 2861 (MDPI, open access).

2025 · Nicosia, Cyprus

Study visit to the urban micro-farms of Nicosia

To develop the business model for the living labs, we visited our partners in Cyprus. We saw two urban micro-farms in Nicosia, where fresh-water scarcity is a major challenge and efficient management of this resource remains essential for urban farming in the region.

Many thanks to our hosts, Vassilis Litskas (Cyprus University of Technology, Department of Agricultural Sciences, Biotechnology and Food Science) and Dimitrios Sarris (Research Director, KES Research Centre), for their hospitality and for a very valuable exchange of knowledge!

2025 · Shanghai, China

FEED4FOOD at the EPAM 2025 conference

Dr George Zaimes of Democritus University of Thrace (DUTh), Greece, presented FEED4FOOD at the 15th International Conference on Environmental and Public Health Issues in Asian Mega-cities (EPAM 2025) in Shanghai.

His talk, “Food Sustainability & Climate Resilience through Urban Gardens”, showed how FEED4FOOD promotes inclusive, sustainable and community-oriented urban food systems that support vulnerable groups and strengthen the climate resilience of European cities. Dr Zaimes also handed out the project brochure.

21–23 March 2025 · Bucharest

Workshop “Innovation and Governance in Food Systems”

The University of Bucharest hosted the annual meeting of the FEED4FOOD partners. Over three days, researchers and partner representatives from all project countries discussed innovation and governance in urban food systems, and participants received certificates of attendance.

Facilitator: Assoc. Prof. Daniel Constantin Diaconu. Scientific committee: Daniel Peptenatu, Lia van Wesenbeeck, Magdalena Platis, Adrian Tișcovschi, Cristian Constantin Drăghici, Petre Brețcan.

November 2024 · Bucharest

FEED4FOOD presented at two conferences in Bucharest

The team presented the project at the University of Bucharest's Annual Research Results Conference (3rd edition, 25–29 November 2024) and at the 3rd International Conference “Re-Shaping Territories, Environment and Societies: New Challenges for Geography” (15–16 November 2024).
